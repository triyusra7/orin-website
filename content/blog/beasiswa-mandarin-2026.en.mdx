Studying abroad sounds expensive — yet for China and Taiwan, the scholarship routes are among the most accessible in the world. Here's the full map for the 2026/2027 academic year.

## Scholarships for China

### CSC — Chinese Government Scholarship

The most popular award from the Chinese government. Coverage can be **full**: tuition waived, free dormitory, insurance, plus a monthly stipend (around RMB 2,500 for bachelor's, more for master's/PhD).

- **Typical requirements:** high-school diploma (for bachelor's, max age 25), transcripts, recommendation letters, a study plan, and HSK at the program's required level.
- **Two routes:** through the **Chinese Embassy in Jakarta** (Type A) or **directly via a university** (Type B). Applying through both is allowed — and doubles your chances.
- **Timeline:** applications generally open **December–April**, with results around July.

### University & provincial scholarships

Nearly every major university runs its own awards — from 25% tuition discounts to full rides. Provincial-government scholarships (Jiangsu and Zhejiang, for example) are less competitive than CSC. Never pin your hopes on a single door.

## Scholarships for Taiwan

### MOE Taiwan Scholarship

Taiwan's Ministry of Education award for degree study (bachelor's to PhD). It subsidizes tuition up to NT$40,000 per semester plus a living allowance of **NT$15,000/month (bachelor's)** or **NT$20,000/month (graduate)**.

- **Requirements:** admission to (or application at) a Taiwanese university, solid academics, and **TOCFL** for Mandarin-taught programs (English-taught programs accept English proficiency instead).
- **Timeline:** apply via **TETO Jakarta**, usually open **February–March**.

### Huayu Enrichment Scholarship (HES)

Specifically for **Mandarin language study** — 3, 6, 9, or 12 months at a university language center, at NT$25,000 per month. Perfect if you want to deepen your Mandarin before committing to a full degree, or for a productive gap year.

## Tips to make your file stand out

1. **Start 12 months before the deadline.** An HSK/TOCFL certificate can't be crammed in a month.
2. **Write a specific study plan.** Explain why that major, why that university, and what you'll do after graduating — avoid templates.
3. **Choose recommenders who actually know you.** A teacher who has seen your work beats an impressive title.
4. **Get documents in order early:** legalized diploma and transcripts, plus sworn translations.
5. **Apply through more than one route.** CSC + a university award + MOE is allowed — just be honest when it's time to choose.
